What it is

A picture of where your attention actually is

Most of what tires you out is not the work in front of you — it is everything running in the background. The argument you keep replaying. The letter you have not answered. The comparison that flares up every time you open your phone. One Current gives each of those a line of its own, so instead of a vague weight you see exactly what is open, how strongly it pulls, and how long it has been running.

One main line

Your life, moving through Now. Everything on it is where you actually are.

Threads

Anything that split off and keeps pulling — a person, a fear, a task, a season of life. Each has a loudness, and the line shows it: louder threads run thicker and more restless.

Loudness

Threads left undecided grow louder on their own, day by day — just like real worries. Any honest decision quiets them again.

Integration

Threads are not deleted or suppressed. When one has given you what it can, it curves back into your main line and travels with you.

Seven living themes

From quiet Riverbed to pomeranian pups, koi ponds, balloons, cats, anglerfish, ghosts and dragons — each open thread becomes a small creature that settles when you decide.

In your language

The whole app speaks English or Spanish — every word it says changes, while your own words stay exactly as you wrote them.

How it works

The whole loop takes about a minute

One Current is not a place to spend time. It is a place to make one small, honest decision and leave.

1 Name what is pulling at you

Tap + and write it down in your own words. A new thread appears on the timeline immediately — roughly when it started, colored, with the strength of its pull.

That alone changes something: the pull now has a shape and a place, instead of circling in your head. And the timeline grows with you — it scrolls to hold as many threads as your life has open.

Every open thread stays visible until you decide what it needs.

2 Say how loud it is

Tap a thread and the sheet opens just a little: its name and one slider — how loud is this thread right now? Set it and go; that touch is not a commitment to do anything.

Or skip the sheet entirely: press the thread and slide your thumb up or down, and its loudness dials right there on the timeline.

Loudness is honest over time, too. A thread left undecided grows louder day by day, and the line shows it — thicker, more restless — until you give it a decision.

One slider first. Everything else stays folded away.

3 Decide what it needs today

When you want to do more, pull the sheet up and answer one question: what does this thread need from you now? While you decide, your main line leans over to meet the thread you chose, so the two read together above the sheet — nothing else moves.

Act — take one small step. Integrate — bring back what still matters. Note — add what just happened. Or say honestly: nothing can be done about it right now — and that counts as a decision too.

Four honest answers. No forms, no scoring.

4 The line changes

Your decisions show up on the timeline itself. Steps you chose appear ahead of Now on your main line. Threads you decided on today are marked. A thread you integrate curves back into the main line and stays part of your history.

The Actions view gathers it into one glance: which threads still need a decision today, and which are already settled.

Undecided above, decided below — nothing more to track.

5 Go deeper only when you want to

Every thread also has a quieter door: understand this thread. There you can name what from it still belongs to you now — confidence, rest, connection, closure — compare where it began with where you are, and notice what feels less available while it stays open.

All of it is optional. Nothing here is required.

Take what is still yours; leave the rest where it happened.

6 See how gathered you are

A small chip in the corner shows your wholeness — how many threads are pulling on you, and how loudly. When you are scattered, it tells you plainly, and points at the one decision that would gather you most right now.

The same loudness flows into your history: day by day you can see which feelings your open threads were holding, and how deciding on them gave the energy back.

Not a score to optimize — a weather report, and one next step.

Why it helps

Built around a few quiet convictions

One Current is not a task manager and not a mood tracker. It sits somewhere calmer: a way to stop carrying everything at once.

Seeing it is half the relief

An open loop you can see is lighter than one you carry blind. The timeline turns background noise into a finite, visible set of lines.

Every decision counts

Taking one small step counts. So does honestly deciding that nothing can be done today. Rest is a decision, not a failure.

Integration, not suppression

Threads are never crossed out or locked away. When one completes for now, you choose what comes back with you — and if it returns later, you meet the new version of it.

Small honest steps

The app never asks for a plan. It asks for the smallest honest step — send one message, read the letter once, say one true sentence.

A minute, not an evening

Every screen has one purpose and a handful of words. You decide, the line changes, and you go back to your life.

Support when it is heavy

Some threads are too heavy to handle alone. One Current says so, plainly, and makes reaching for support one of the normal answers.

Privacy

Everything stays in your browser

What you write in One Current is stored only on your device, in this browser. There is no account, no server, no analytics. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can export your data as a file whenever you want, and delete everything just as easily.

Add One Current to your phone

One Current installs straight from Safari — no app store needed:

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button .
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It opens full screen with its own icon, and everything you write stays on your device.

One Current installs straight from your browser — no app store needed:

  1. Open the app in your browser.
  2. Open the browser menu (⋮ or the install icon in the address bar).
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

It opens in its own window with its own icon, and everything you write stays on your device.

You are already using the installed app — One Current is on your home screen.
